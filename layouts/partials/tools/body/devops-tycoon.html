How to play
  • Traffic grows on its own. Every successful request earns money; every server, the database and each add-on costs money each tick.
  • Watch CPU and the database. Past their limits, latency and the error rate climb and revenue falls while costs stay the same.
  • Buy capacity early. A second server only pays off fully once you own the load balancer.
  • Answer incidents. A quick fix is cheap but temporary; buying the matching upgrade fixes the cause for good.
  • Stay in the black. Bankruptcy or ruined reputation ends the run. Use Reset to start over.
