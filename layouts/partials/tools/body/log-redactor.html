Runs locally in your browser. Inputs are not uploaded or saved. Original log text stays visible here; do not paste it while screen sharing.

Exact-text entries are literal, not regex. Minimum 3 characters per entry; maximum 100 distinct entries and 10,000 characters total. Blank lines are ignored; spaces are significant.

Sensitive fields redact the rest of their line, so the output may no longer be valid JSON. An incomplete quoted, empty, or object-valued sensitive field, or an unclosed private-key block, hides the remaining log. Detection can miss secrets and can redact harmless text. It is not an anonymization guarantee.
