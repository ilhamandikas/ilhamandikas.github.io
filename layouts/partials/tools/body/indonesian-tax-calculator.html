Calculate Indonesian tax

Experimental · partial coverage. Runs locally in your browser. Amounts are not uploaded or saved by this tool. Independent calculator, not affiliated with DJP.

The reference catalogue contains all 193 object options from the downloaded DJP calculator, not every national or historical filing code. Income-tax coverage ends on 2024-12-31; selected domestic VAT scenarios also cover 2025. Unsupported dates and scenarios are blocked. Check coverage before relying on a result.

For annual Article 17 tax, use the tax-year end date (31 December). Construction uses the payment date, not the contract date. Date alone does not determine whether a transaction is taxable.

Object names and codes are a reference snapshot, not a period-validated filing registry. A calculated transaction does not validate its displayed code for historical filing. English descriptions are explanatory translations; official Indonesian wording is retained.

Enter whole rupiah using digits only. No commas, decimal fractions or exponent notation. Taxable bases must already exclude legally permitted exclusions; this tool does not determine them.

Choose a scenario, enter its amount and confirm the assumptions.

Tax calculation

Calculation stepValue

Arithmetic is exact, using integer fractions rather than floating-point money. Recurring amounts are shown as exact fractions of a rupiah. Only statutory PKP rounding is applied. All tax figures are before tax-document rounding. Article 31E allocations are not rounded at intermediate steps; gross-up is algebraic, not a rounded payroll reconciliation. This is not a tax return or a determination of liability.
