Runs locally in your browser. Certificate input is not uploaded or saved. Paste certificates only—never private keys.

Paste a certificate to inspect its fields.

Up to 20 PEM certificates, maximum 1,000,000 input characters. Dates are shown in UTC and checked against your device clock. This does not check signatures, hostname matches, trust chains, or revocation, and does not contact a server. A bundle is decoded in input order, not verified as a chain.
