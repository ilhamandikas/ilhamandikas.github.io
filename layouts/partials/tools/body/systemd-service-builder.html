Runs locally. Inputs are not uploaded or saved. Generates a system-level service for a foreground application under an existing non-root account; nothing is installed or executed here.

Do not paste a shell command. Spaces inside a line belong to that single argument. Dollar signs and percent signs are escaped as literals: no variable expansion, pipes, redirects, or shell evaluation.

Network ordering is not a guarantee of connectivity. Hardening is optional: it prevents gaining privileges and isolates /tmp and /var/tmp, which can change application behavior. Review paths, permissions, and environment-file syntax on your server.
