Reading the numbers
  • Level is the loudness right now in dBFS, where 0 is the loudest a digital signal can be and silence sits near -60.
  • Peak is the loudest moment since listening started. A peak that reaches 0 means the input is clipping, and turning the gain down will sound better than any repair afterwards.
  • A bar that barely moves usually means the wrong input device is selected, or the gain is set low in the system sound settings rather than in the browser.
  • Speech that sits between about -24 and -12 dBFS is comfortable for a call or a recording.
  • Record captures the same signal the meter shows. A fresh recording replaces the last one, and Discard throws it away without a trace on disk.
