Runs locally in your browser. Inputs are not uploaded or saved. Pasted inputs remain visible; result values are hidden by default.

Single-line KEY=value assignments only. Comments and optional export prefixes are ignored. Outer quotes are removed; escapes and ${VARIABLE} references are compared literally, not expanded. Keys are case-sensitive. Duplicate keys and multiline values must be resolved first. Maximum 1,000,000 characters per input.
