A percentage means “out of 100”. **20%** means 20 out of every 100. [Percentage Calculator](/tools/percentage-calculator/) uses two values to answer several different percentage questions at once.

## Start with easy numbers

Set **Value A** to `20` and **Value B** to `100`. Read these rows separately:

- **20% of 100:** `20`. This asks for a piece of 100.
- **20 is what % of 100:** `20%`. This asks what share 20 is of 100.
- **Change from 20 to 100:** `400%`. This asks how much the value **increased relative to where it started**. It grew by 80, and 80 is four times the starting value 20.

The last answer is **not** 80% because the starting value matters. To find a decrease, swap the two values: a change from 100 down to 20 is `-80%`. This does not undo a 400% rise; each change uses a different starting value.

## If you see an unusual result

Check which number you put in A and B, then read the whole label beside each output. Different rows answer different questions. With zero as the starting value, a percentage change cannot be calculated by dividing by zero; the tool shows a dash for that row. Do not treat a percentage without its starting value as enough information to make a decision.

## Where your input goes

Processing runs in your browser. The tool does not upload your input to ilham.dev.

## Questions you might have

### Why do a percentage rise and fall not cancel out?

Because they are relative to different bases. A 50% rise followed by a 50% fall leaves you at 75% of where you started, not back at the beginning.
