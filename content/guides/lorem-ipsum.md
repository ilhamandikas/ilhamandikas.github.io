Generate placeholder text as paragraphs, sentences or words, starting from the classic Latin passage or a shuffled word pool. It is for filling a layout before the real copy exists.

## Open the tool

[Use Lorem Ipsum](/tools/lorem-ipsum/).

## Where your input goes

Processing runs in your browser. The tool does not upload your input to ilham.dev.

## Questions you might have

### Why not just write the real copy first?

Because placeholder text makes it obvious what is a layout problem and what is a content problem, and it stops a review turning into an edit of the words instead of the design.

### Is the Latin real?

It is a mangled excerpt of Cicero, which is the traditional joke. It is not meaningful Latin.
