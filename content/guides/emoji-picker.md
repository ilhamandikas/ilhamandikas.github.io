Search and copy emoji by name, with the whole set available rather than the subset your keyboard happens to offer.

## Open the tool

[Use Emoji Picker](/tools/emoji-picker/).

## Where your input goes

Processing runs in your browser. The tool does not upload your input to ilham.dev.

## Questions you might have

### Why do some emoji render as boxes?

The font on your system does not include them. The character is still correct and will render wherever the font supports it.
