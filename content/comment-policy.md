Comments are welcome. A few simple rules keep things readable:

- **Be decent.** No personal attacks, harassment, hate speech, or threats. Disagree with
  the idea, not the person.
- **Stay on topic.** Keep it related to the post.
- **No spam.** No ads or unrelated self-promotion. Linking your own relevant work is
  fine — just say what it is.
- **Nothing illegal.** No unlawful, defamatory, or malicious content, and no posting
  other people's private information.
- **I moderate.** I may edit, hide, or delete any comment and block anyone, without
  prior notice. Spam is usually removed automatically.

A few things worth knowing:

- **Comments are public.** Whatever you post can be indexed by search engines and read
  by anyone, so don't share anything sensitive. You keep ownership of your words;
  posting here just lets me display them.
- **They live on GitHub.** Comments are stored as GitHub Discussions in a public
  repository and shown through giscus. Posting requires a GitHub account, so GitHub's
  terms and privacy policy also apply — see the [Privacy Statement](/privacy/).
- **Opinions belong to the commenter.** I don't verify or endorse what others write and
  am not responsible for it.

See something that breaks these rules, or have a question? Email
[hai@ilham.dev](mailto:hai@ilham.dev).
