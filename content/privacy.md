This Privacy Statement explains how **ilham.dev** ("the Site"), operated by Ilham
Andika ("I", "me", or "my"), handles information when you visit. I aim to collect as
little as reasonably possible and to be transparent about what is handled.

## 1. Information I Collect

The Site is a static website and does not require an account. I do not intentionally
collect personal information that identifies you, except in the following cases:

- **Information you send me.** If you email me or otherwise contact me, I receive the
  information you choose to share (such as your name, email address, and message).
- **Technical data.** Like most websites, the infrastructure that serves the Site may
  process standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type,
  requested URL, and timestamps, in order to deliver the Site and protect it from
  abuse.

## 2. How Information Is Used

- to respond to your messages and inquiries;
- to operate, maintain, and secure the Site;
- to diagnose technical problems and prevent abuse or attacks;
- to comply with applicable legal obligations.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run any
analytics. However, third-party services used to operate the Site (see below) may set
cookies or similar identifiers as part of their normal operation. In particular,
comments on posts are stored as GitHub Discussions and shown through giscus, an
embedded frame served from giscus.app that may set cookies when GitHub handles your
sign-in or session. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services

- **GitHub Pages** hosts the Site. When the Site is served, GitHub may process
  technical data such as your IP address and user agent in its logs as part of
  delivering the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
- **Cloudflare** provides DNS (and, where enabled, security and delivery in front of
  the Site). Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses as a data
  processor in order to provide these services. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for
  details.
- **giscus and GitHub** provide the comment system on posts. Comments are stored as
  GitHub Discussions in a public repository, and the comment widget is an embedded
  frame served by giscus.app. When a post page loads, GitHub and giscus may process
  technical data such as your IP address and browser information. Posting a comment
  requires signing in with a GitHub account. See GitHub's privacy statement and the
  giscus documentation for details.
- **Tools that contact third parties.** A few tools reach out to public services to do
  their job, and each one sends only the value you type in. **IP Lookup** sends the
  address to ipwho.is (falling back to freeipapi.com). **DNS Lookup** sends the name to
  Google Public DNS or Cloudflare's DNS-over-HTTPS resolver. **WHOIS Lookup** sends the
  domain to the IANA bootstrap directory, the registry's own RDAP service and rdap.org.
  The request testers (**CORS Checker**, **HTTP Request Tester**, **Webhook Tester**,
  **gRPC Tester** and **WebSocket Tester**) send your request to whatever URL you enter.
  The **JavaScript Playground** loads the Monaco editor from cdn.jsdelivr.net the first
  time you enable it. Every other tool runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing.
- **External links**, for example to GitHub, are governed by those sites' own privacy
  policies.

## 5. Log Data and Retention

Server and security logs may be retained for a limited period as necessary for
security, troubleshooting, and legal compliance, and are then deleted or anonymized.
Information you send me by email is retained only for as long as needed to handle your
request and maintain reasonable records.

## 6. Legal Bases

Where applicable law (such as the GDPR) requires a legal basis, processing is based on
my legitimate interests in operating and securing the Site, your consent where you
contact me, and compliance with legal obligations.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict
the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or request data
portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact me at the address below. You may
also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 8. Security

I take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled
through the Site, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access. No
method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute
security.

## 9. International Transfers

The Site is served through a global content delivery network, so technical data may be
processed in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are
used for such transfers.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and I do not knowingly
collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal
information, please contact me so I can remove it.

## 11. Changes to This Statement

I may update this Privacy Statement from time to time. When I do, I will revise the
"Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
